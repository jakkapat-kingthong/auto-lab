# Never Look Back: Understanding Persistence in 3D Object Memory from Egocentric Videos

> ArXiv | 2026-10-08 | paper 2

As we move through the world and carry out everyday tasks, we encounter objects that may become relevant only later. We are capable of recalling where we left something or what was inside a container, even without knowing we would need it later.
