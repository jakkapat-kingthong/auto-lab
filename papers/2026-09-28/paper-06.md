# User Model Extraction via Belief Self-Distillation

> ArXiv | 2026-09-28 | paper 6

Large language models (LLMs) implicitly infer attributes of their users and adapt their behavior accordingly, yet these beliefs remain difficult to inspect and causally manipulate. We introduce Belief Self-Distillation (BSD), a unified read-write framework that bridges linear and causal probing by learning a compact user representation that can be both decoded and written back into the model.
