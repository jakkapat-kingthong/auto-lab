In this paper, we explore a novel task of Multimodal Unsupervised Continual Post-Training (MU-CPT), enabling deployed MLLMs to continually evolve from streaming unlabeled data. Existing unsupervised post-training methods for MLLMs typically optimize target tokens uniformly, overlooking their heterogeneous visual dependence (VD).
