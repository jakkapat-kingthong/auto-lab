# Agentic Detection of Online Conspiracies

> ArXiv | 2026-09-26 | paper 4

Conspiratorial discourse on social media is not always expressed through explicit claims or stable lexical markers. The same surface content may express endorsement, legitimate concerns, criticism, satire, or mockery.
