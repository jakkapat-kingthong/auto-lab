# Scaling Properties of Text Conditioning in Visual Generation

> ArXiv | 2026-08-03 | paper 2

We study empirical scaling properties for text conditioning in visual generation. Such properties have rarely been measured because diffusion loss does not scale with the number of tokens in natural-language prompts.
