# EgoGeneralist: Unifying Egocentric Action-Video Tasks through Video-Native Modeling

> ArXiv | 2026-11-07 | paper 6

Modern video models possess strong visual priors, yet do not natively represent human action in metric 3D space. We present EgoGeneralist, which adapts pretrained video generators to unify action-conditioned egocentric video generation, pose estimation, and joint action-video generation.
