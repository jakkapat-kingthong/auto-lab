# Rethinking the design space of Vanilla Pixel Diffusion Transformers

> ArXiv | 2026-10-19 | paper 2

Pixel-space diffusion models have recently re-emerged as a compelling alternative to latent diffusion, avoiding the lossy latent bottleneck and separately trained autoencoder.
