# A Decentralized Partially Observable Team Decision Methodology with Delayed Information Sharing

> ArXiv | 2026-09-23 | paper 4

We study decentralized partially observable team decision problems with low-rank latent dynamics and unknown system models. The proposed framework combines team-theoretic equivalence with low-rank model representations to address cooperative decision-making in partially observable Markov decision processes without prior knowledge of the transition model.
