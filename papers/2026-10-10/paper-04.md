# CSF: Contextual Safety Filtering for Motion Generators

> ArXiv | 2026-10-10 | paper 4

Text-conditioned motion generators produce trackable whole-body motion, but they have no notion of scene-dependent safety: the same action may target an object or a person. Existing safeguards either inspect the prompt, require labeled motion data, or enforce geometric constraints; therefore, they do not directly account for how scene context changes a motion's meaning.
