# DreamTrue: Action-Faithful Robot World Model with Counterfactual Post-Training

> ArXiv | 2026-10-10 | paper 3

We present DreamTrue, a multi-view, cross-embodiment robot world model for action-faithful and physically plausible video prediction. Training such a model on existing robot datasets faces two obstacles: imprecise calibration can impair action following, while limited coverage of unsuccessful interactions can bias predictions toward successful outcomes.
