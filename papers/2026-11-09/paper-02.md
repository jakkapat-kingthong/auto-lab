# On Agentic Manipulation Policies

> ArXiv | 2026-11-09 | paper 2

Recent work on robot manipulation has focused on specialist policies trained on demonstrations. We show that generalist AI models can control manipulators more reliably than specialist policies such as vision-language-action models (VLAs) and world-action models (WAMs).
