# Non-penetrating World Models

> ArXiv | 2026-11-23 | paper 4

We study a new problem of preventing object penetration in interactive video world models. To understand how existing models handle this, we introduce NoPeBench, a benchmark that evaluates how world models respond to physical obstacles.
