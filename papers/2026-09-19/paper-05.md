# SplashSplat: Reconstructing Splashing Liquids from Real-World Multi-View Videos

> ArXiv | 2026-09-19 | paper 5

A splash lives for a fraction of a second: sheets tear into ligaments and droplets, appearance is view-dependent and nearly textureless, and little persists long enough to track. Reconstruction research has consequently focused on smoke, synthetic liquids, or gently deforming surfaces. To our knowledge, no synchronized multi-view dataset of splashing liquids exists.
