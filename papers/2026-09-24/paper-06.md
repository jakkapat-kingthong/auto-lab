# Even Sharper Bounds for Transductive Learning and Its Applications

> ArXiv | 2026-09-24 | paper 6

We introduce Sharper Transductive Local Complexity (STLC), a localized complexity method for transductive learning under uniform sampling without replacement. The construction starts from a Bernstein-type concentration inequality for the supremum of the test--train empirical process.
