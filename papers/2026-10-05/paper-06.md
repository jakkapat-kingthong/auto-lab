# EyeRobot 2.0: Active Gaze for Precise Manipulation without Wrist Cameras

> ArXiv | 2026-10-05 | paper 6

Inspired by human vision, we introduce a framework using active gaze to enable fine-grained bimanual manipulation with only a single stereo camera. EyeRobot 2.0 physically attends to a 3D fixation point in the scene by swiveling two eye viewpoints to center their gaze on it.
