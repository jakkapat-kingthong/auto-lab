# Less Decoder is More Encoder: Geometric Representation Learning from Novel View Synthesis

> ArXiv | 2026-10-05 | paper 1

This paper examines the role of Novel View Synthesis (NVS) in geometric representation learning. In principle, NVS should reason about 3D scene structure, thereby enabling transferable multi-view geometric representations. Yet, existing encoder-based NVS methods yield poor representations.
