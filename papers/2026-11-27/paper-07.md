# Tokenisation is not in APX

> ArXiv | 2026-11-27 | paper 7

In this paper, we prove that, unless P=NP, no polynomial-time algorithm can find a tokeniser whose objective is within any constant factor of the optimum, meaning that tokenisation is not in the APX complexity class.
