# A Mesh for Every Moment

> ArXiv | 2026-11-27 | paper 6

We present MEMO (a Mesh for Every MOment), a method that reconstructs the geometry of complete dynamic scenes, captured by multiple synchronized cameras, and extracts a mesh at every frame.
