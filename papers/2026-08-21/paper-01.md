# Information on trajectories: martingales and random times

> ArXiv | 2026-08-21 | paper 1

Accounting for information flow on the path space of trajectories of a nonnegative martingale yields exact variational identities for it, even at arbitrary random times. This recovers the widely used classical concentration inequalities, from Ville to PAC-Bayes, and measures what each one discards.
