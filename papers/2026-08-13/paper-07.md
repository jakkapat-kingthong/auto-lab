# A Framework for Designing Reward Functions: From Objectives to Features to Human-Aligned Reward Functions

> ArXiv | 2026-08-13 | paper 7

We present a formal process to enable non-experts to instantiate and iterate on human-aligned reward functions, i.e. reward functions that adhere to a given preference ordering over trajectories.
