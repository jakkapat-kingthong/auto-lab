# Point2Part: Unified 3D Partitioning from Point Prompts

> ArXiv | 2026-09-30 | paper 1

Existing 3D part decomposition methods do not necessarily partition the original shape into non-overlapping parts that collectively cover the entire shape, allowing overlaps or gaps that hinder downstream part-level applications.
