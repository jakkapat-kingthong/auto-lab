# Training Reasoning Models in Simulation to Natively Use Real Robots

> ArXiv | 2026-11-16 | paper 3

Robot-use agents (RUAs) have emerged as a promising approach to general-purpose robot control. However, strong performance has so far been achieved only by frontier proprietary models such as GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable 5.2, whereas open-weight models remain substantially less capable.
