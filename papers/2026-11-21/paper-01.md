# Posterior Matching Distillation

> ArXiv | 2026-11-21 | paper 1

Diffusion distillation accelerates image and video generation by compressing a pretrained teacher's iterative sampling process into a few network evaluations. Distribution-based methods achieve this by matching the student's output distribution to the teacher's.
